1. Create a Twitch app

  1. Go to dev.twitch.tv/console/apps and click Register Your Application.
  2. OAuth Redirect URL:  ·  Category: Chat Bot  ·  Client Type: Confidential.
  3. Open the app (Manage), copy the Client ID, click New Secret and copy it.
  4. Put both in the .env file in the project folder:
    TWITCH_CLIENT_ID=…
    TWITCH_CLIENT_SECRET=…
  5. Restart the app and come back here.
